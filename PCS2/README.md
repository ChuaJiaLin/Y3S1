# Reflection

The Professional Communication Skills 2 course provided valuable exposure to essential professional communication techniques, particularly in proposal writing, pitching, negotiation, and formal group discussions. The course offered a structured approach to developing communication competencies required in both academic and workplace environments.

Through various speaking and writing activities, significant improvement was achieved in presenting ideas clearly, organizing arguments logically, and delivering effective professional presentations. Emphasis on clarity, structure, and audience awareness contributed to stronger communication performance.

The proposal writing and pitching assessments played a key role in enhancing the ability to express ideas persuasively and confidently. These activities also strengthened critical thinking, as well as the ability to analyze information and present it in a convincing and professional manner.

In addition, negotiation exercises and role-play activities contributed to the development of teamwork, interpersonal communication, and adaptability. Active participation in discussions and collaborative problem-solving encouraged confidence and improved the ability to respond effectively in dynamic situations.

The course also increased awareness of the importance of effective communication in professional settings, highlighting its role in career development and organizational success.

Overall, the course successfully enhanced professional speaking, writing, and presentation skills, while strengthening confidence and competence in formal communication and group interaction.
